Your surf lesson: start on the sand, finish on a wave

Every lesson begins on the beach: a warm-up, the pop-up practiced on a board in the sand, and how to read the whitewater. Then you head into the water with your instructor beside you, pushing you into waves until you are standing on your own.

It's for every level. If it's your first time, the goal is your first ride. If you've surfed before, we work on what's next: paddling, timing, and choosing your waves.

Before you come

  • Know how to swim.
  • Be ready to swallow some salt water.
  • Wear a wetsuit top or a long-sleeve T-shirt.
  • Put on sunscreen before you arrive.

Why surf in Jacó, Costa Rica

Jacó is one of the best places in Costa Rica to learn to surf. Its long beach break on the central Pacific coast has consistent waves all year, warm water, and room for beginners and experienced surfers alike.

It's also a real surf town. Surf shops, cafés and restaurants line the streets a few steps from the sand, the locals are friendly, and everything you need is close by. After your lesson you can rent a board, grab a meal, and paddle back out.

Dry season, December to April

Sunny days and offshore winds that keep the waves clean and consistent. This is peak season, and the best time to learn.

Green season, May to November

Occasional rain and thunderstorms. The waves are still good, with fewer people in the water and lower prices around town.

Questions people ask

I've never surfed. Can I still take a surf lesson in Jacó?

Yes. Lessons are for every level, and they start on the sand before you ever paddle out. Experienced surfers are welcome too; we adjust the lesson to your level.

How much does a surf lesson in Jacó cost?

A two-hour lesson costs $45 per person for big groups, $49 per person for a shared lesson and $60 for a private lesson. The surfboard is always included.

Do I need to know how to swim?

Yes. You'll be in the ocean with a board leashed to your ankle, so you need to be comfortable swimming on your own.

What should I bring to my surf lesson?

Swimwear, sunscreen, and a wetsuit top or a long-sleeve T-shirt to protect you from the sun and the board. We provide the surfboard.

When is the best time of year to surf in Jacó, Costa Rica?

December to April brings dry, sunny weather and clean waves. May to November has more rain but fewer crowds and lower prices, and the waves are still there.
